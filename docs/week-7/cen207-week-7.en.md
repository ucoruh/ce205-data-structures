This week there is no new topic: each team demonstrates the **C implementation** of the term project (RAP1) and submits the midterm report.

!!! info "What to bring"
    - Your project running on **Windows and WSL/Linux** (build with CMake from a clean clone).
    - GoogleTest results with **100% coverage** and the Doxygen PDF.
    - The midterm report (design, why each structure, Big-O and measurement tables).

- **Scope:** requirements V1–V6 (weeks 1–6) — see the [Project Guide](../project-guide/index.md), sections 5 and 8.
- **Demo:** about 10 minutes per team: live run, one operation explained line by line with a box-and-arrow drawing, questions to every member.
- **Next week:** Quiz-1 (weeks 1–6) in the midterm exam week.
